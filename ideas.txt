RDMA (Remote Direct Memory Access) is literally the ability for my Network Card to reach into your RAM and write data without your CPU ever knowing.

Enter RoCE (RDMA over Converged Ethernet)
"Infiniband" is the rich cousin of networking—expensive, specialized cables. RoCE (pronounced "Rocky") is the blue-collar hero. It lets us do that teleportation trick over the standard Ethernet cables you already have in your house.

Why this is the "Holy Grail" for Project Chi:

When you "toss" a build to the Jetson:

Quest 3 (The Scanner): Detects the throw vector.

Gemma (The Archivist on Pi): Calculates the intersection. "Target: Jetson."

The 4080 (The Muscle): Compiles the build. It produces a binary in its RAM.

The RoCE Magic: The 4080's Network Card sees the Jetson's memory address. It DMA's (Direct Memory Access) the binary straight across the wire into the Jetson's RAM.

The Jetson: Suddenly has the data. Its CPU wakes up only after the data is there to say "Job Done."
